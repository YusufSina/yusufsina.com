I’ve published a new version of my personal website to start 2026 with a cleaner slate.

This site is meant to be simple: a place to share what I’m building, what I’m learning, and how I think about engineering, fintech, and product. No noise, no polish for the sake of polish — just clarity.

Over the past years, I’ve worked mostly on backend-heavy systems in fintech and crypto. In 2026, I want to be more intentional about **building end-to-end products**, sharing ideas earlier, and documenting the journey — not just the outcomes.

This blog will be a space for:
- short technical notes
- lessons from building
- product and startup thoughts
- things I wish I had written down earlier

The goal isn’t perfection. It’s consistency.

Here’s to building, learning, and shipping in 2026 🚀
