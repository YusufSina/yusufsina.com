For most of my career, I've built things quietly.

I worked on backend-heavy systems in fintech and crypto, shipped side projects, and started building MeclisX end to end. But very little of that ever made it outside my own head or a GitHub repo. If you didn't work with me directly, you probably had no idea what I was working on.

I've decided to change that. From now on, I want to be **more active on LinkedIn** and more visible in the industry.

---

## Why now

When I launched this site at the start of 2026, I wrote that I wanted to share ideas earlier and document the journey, not just the outcomes. The site helped, but a blog only reaches the people who already know where to look.

LinkedIn is where most of the people I work with, learn from and want to build with already are. Staying silent there means missing conversations I'd actually enjoy being part of.

There's also a simpler reason: I'm building a product now. Being visible isn't about vanity anymore. It's how people find out the product exists, and how I meet the people who can make it better.

---

## What I'll share

I don't want to post for the sake of posting. The plan is to share things I'd find useful if someone else wrote them:

- **What I'm building:** progress on MeclisX and other projects, including the parts that don't work
- **Lessons from building:** going from backend engineer to building a whole product, from the mobile app to the web panel
- **Short technical notes:** small things I figured out and wish I had found written down somewhere
- **What I'm reading and thinking about:** finance, investing, product and startups

---

## The rule I'm giving myself

Consistency over perfection. A short, honest post every week is better than a polished one every few months.

The longer pieces will keep living here on the blog. LinkedIn is where I'll share the shorter versions, and where I hope the conversation happens.

If you're reading this, [let's connect on LinkedIn](https://linkedin.com/in/yusufsina). I'd love to hear what you're building too.
