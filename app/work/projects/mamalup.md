## The idea

**Mamalüp** is a recipe and sharing community for mothers whose babies are moving on to solid foods.

Around six months, every parent hits the same daily question: *what do I give the baby today?* The answers are scattered across Instagram accounts, WhatsApp groups and notes on the fridge, and most of them don't say which age a recipe is for. Mamalüp puts it in one Turkish-language app: recipes sorted by the baby's age, a way to cook with what's already in the kitchen, and other mothers to learn from.

## What it does

- **Recipes by the baby's age.** The home screen greets you with your baby's name and age and a suggestion for today. Recipes are grouped for 6, 8, 10 and 12 months and by meal (breakfast, lunch, dinner, fruit), and you can save favourites to your own recipe notebook.
- **"What's in my pantry?"** Type in what you have at home and the recipes that match the most ingredients come first.
- **First 100 foods.** Tick off each food your baby has tasted, group by group, and see what's still missing.
- **Badges and levels.** Cooking, sharing and tracking earn badges and move you up through five levels, from *Yeni Mutfak* to *Mamalüp Bilgesi*.
- **Feed and follows.** Follow other mothers and see their new recipes and badges in one feed.
- **Share your own recipe.** Mothers post recipes with photos. Every recipe and photo is reviewed by the team before it goes live, and anything can be reported or its author blocked.
- **Guide and tip of the day.** Short reads about the solid-food stage and a new tip every day. The app is clear that this is general information, not medical advice.

## How it's built

I built Mamalüp end to end, from the design to the App Store listing: a React Native mobile app, a Python backend and a Next.js website.

## Status

Mamalüp is live on the App Store in Türkiye, and version 1.1 adds follower lists, searching for other mothers and badges on profiles. Learn more at [mamalup.com](https://mamalup.com).
