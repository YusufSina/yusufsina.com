## The idea

**MeclisX** is a community platform that brings the branches and members of business associations and similar organisations together in one place.

Most of these communities run on scattered WhatsApp groups and Excel lists. MeclisX replaces that with a single product, designed to strengthen communication between members and keep the community active. I founded it and build it end to end.

## For members: the mobile app

- **Events.** Members see their branch's upcoming, ongoing and completed events and let organisers know whether they are going, not sure, or not going. Venues open directly on a map.
- **Member directory.** Members can find and reach other members and their companies, with each profile showing company and role.
- **Cross-trade and collaboration.** Members share business and partnership opportunities with the rest of the community.

## For branch managers: the web panel

Branch managers use a web panel to manage members, groups, events and tasks, and follow participation through reports. That gives the management team visibility into how engaged the community really is, without chasing replies across chat groups and spreadsheets.

## Status

MeclisX is live and being used by a pilot community. Learn more at [meclisx.com](https://meclisx.com).
