## The idea

Investing advice on social media is mostly opinions. **X of Wall Street** asked a simpler question: what if you could see the portfolios behind the posts?

It is a social platform where investors share their portfolios, follow each other and learn from what others actually hold. I built it in 2025 as a solo side project, mixing my interest in finance with product and engineering.

![X of Wall Street profile and portfolio pages](/images/xows/xows1.png)

## What it does

- **Portfolio sharing.** Publish your holdings and let others see your strategy.
- **Following.** Follow investors whose approach you want to learn from and see their updates in a feed.
- **Performance tracking.** Track how a portfolio performs over time and compare strategies.
- **Community.** Discover new ideas through what other people are holding.

## Looking back

I built X of Wall Street end to end on my own, from the data model to the UI. It is no longer online, but it was a useful exercise in designing a social product around financial data, where trust and clarity matter more than features.
