## Overview

X of Wall Street is a web-based social platform designed for investors and traders to share their portfolios, track performance, and connect with like-minded individuals. The platform enables users to showcase their investment strategies, follow successful traders, and learn from the community's collective wisdom.

![X of Wall Street Application Screenshot 2](/images/xows/xows1.png)

## Key Features

### Portfolio Sharing
- Share your investment portfolio with the community
- Display your trading performance and statistics
- Showcase your investment strategy and holdings

### Social Networking
- Follow other traders and investors
- Discover successful investment strategies
- Build connections within the trading community

### Community Learning
- Learn from experienced traders
- Discover new investment opportunities
- Engage with a community of investors

### Performance Tracking
- Track and display portfolio performance
- Share insights and analysis
- Compare strategies with other users

## Platform Purpose

X of Wall Street creates a social layer around investing, making it easier for traders to share knowledge, learn from each other, and build a community around investment strategies. The platform democratizes access to investment insights and creates transparency in trading performance.

## Technology Stack

- **Platform**: Web Application
- **Focus**: Social networking for investors
- **Features**: Portfolio sharing, user following, community engagement
